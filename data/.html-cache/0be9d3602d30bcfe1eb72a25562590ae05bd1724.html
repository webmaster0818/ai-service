[Online] Where to begin with AI implementation? Lessons learned from supporting over 600 companies: the right way to proceed.

# Seminar

"We want to implement AI, but we don't know where to start." This is a common question we hear from business owners who are beginning to consider AI implementation. Contracting for tools, training employees, establishing rules—even if the tasks are clear, getting the order wrong can easily lead to the system being only used by a few people and stalling. In this seminar, Masahiro Chaen, CEO of DigiRise, who has supported over 600 companies, will clearly explain, using real-world examples, what kinds of jobs AI can handle today and how to proceed without making mistakes in equipping the necessary tools, rules, and people. We will also touch upon important considerations regarding AI security, and participants will receive an "AI Security Guidebook" (supervised by DigiRise).
