Sakana MarlinとはWhat is Sakana Marlin

Ultra Deep Researchを担う、
あなたのVirtual CSO。Your Virtual CSO for Ultra Deep Research.

Sakana Marlinは、単なるディープリサーチアシスタントではありません。本来であればCSO（最高戦略責任者）とそのチームが数週間を費やすような、本格的な戦略リサーチを引き受ける自律型エージェントです。Sakana AI独自の技術を基盤に、推論時の計算リソースをスケールさせ、最大8時間に及ぶ連続的かつ自律的な推論を実行。一度テーマを設定すれば、人手を介さずに、仮説の立案・情報収集・Web探索・矛盾の解消を自ら繰り返します。


Sakana Marlinは、要約にとどまりません。
複雑なビジネス環境に潜む因果関係を読み解き、構造化された戦略の選択肢へと整理します。網羅的なリサーチという重い負担と徹底した戦略レポートの作成を引き受けることで、人間は最も価値の高い仕事——「意思決定」そのもの——に集中できます。

Sakana Marlin isn't just another deep research assistant. It is an autonomous agent designed to take on the kind of substantial strategy research that a Chief Strategy Officer and a small team might otherwise spend weeks on.


Instead of generating text in seconds, our focus is on long-horizon, sample-efficient reasoning. Powered by Sakana AI's core reasoning architecture, Sakana Marlin scales up inference-time compute to execute up to eight hours of continuous, autonomous reasoning. Once you set a research topic, it works without further human input: repeatedly forming hypotheses, gathering information, navigating the web, and resolving contradictions on its own.


It does more than summarize. Sakana Marlin maps the causal relationships at work in complex business environments and organizes them into structured strategic options. By taking on the heavy lifting of comprehensive research and delivering exhaustive strategy reports, Sakana Marlin frees you to concentrate on the highest-value work of all: the decisions themselves.

セキュリティ・FAQFAQ
Q1他のリサーチ系AIと何が違うのですか？How does Sakana Marlin differ from other research AI tools?
Sakana MarlinはAB-MCTSにより仮説立案 → 探索 → 検証を自律的に繰り返す点が異なり、単なる情報集約ではなく、複数の仮説を比較検討した上での示唆を返します。意思決定の手前にある「論点設計」までAIに任せたい用途を想定しています。 Sakana Marlin is distinct in its use of AB-MCTS to autonomously iterate through hypothesis formulation → exploration → verification. Rather than merely aggregating information, it compares and evaluates multiple hypotheses to provide deep insights. It is designed for use cases where you want to delegate everything up to "issue framing (agenda setting)" prior to decision-making to the AI.
Q21回の調査にどのくらい時間がかかりますか？How long does a single research task take?
調査テーマの複雑さや探索の深さに応じて変動しますが、目安は約8時間です。Sakana Marlinは即時応答型のリサーチツールではなく、AB-MCTSによって仮説立案・探索・検証を時間をかけて深く行うことで、意思決定に耐える品質のアウトプットを生成します。実行中はいつでもキャンセル可能ですが、クレジットは消費されますのでご注意ください。While it varies depending on the complexity of the research topic and the depth of the exploration, the general guideline is approximately 8 hours. Sakana Marlin is not a real-time, instant-response conversational AI; it is designed to take the time necessary to conduct deep, rigorous hypothesis formulation, exploration, and verification via AB-MCTS to generate high-quality outputs suitable for strategic decision-making. You can cancel at any time during execution, but please note that credits will still be consumed.
Q3どのような調査テーマに向いていますか？逆に、苦手なテーマは？What types of research topics are ideal for Sakana Marlin, and which are not recommended?
公開情報を統合して仮説検証を行う調査に強みがあります（戦略立案、市場・競合分析、技術トレンド評価、規制動向など）。一方、(a) 公開情報がほぼ存在しないニッチ領域、(b) 秒単位のリアルタイム性が求められる用途、(c) 社内非公開データのみで完結する調査、には現状適していません。Sakana Marlin excels at hypothesis validation using aggregated public data, such as strategic planning, market and competitor analysis, tech trend evaluations, and regulatory shifts. Conversely, it is currently not suited for (a) niche sectors with minimal public data, (b) use cases requiring split-second real-time tracking, or (c) research that relies entirely on internal, private data.

Please note that it designed for professional business use cases, and not intended for general consumer use.
