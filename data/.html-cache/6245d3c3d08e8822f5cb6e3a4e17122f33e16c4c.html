About

Sakana AI is a Tokyo-based Frontier AI R&D company founded by David Ha (CEO), Ren Ito (Chairman), and Llion Jones (CTO) in 2023. Our team, behind technologies such as The AI Scientist, Multi-Agent Orchestration Foundation Models, our Namazu LLMs for Japan, and the Darwin Gödel Machine, is constantly pushing the frontier of AI research. Our company is also working with the largest enterprises and with the public sector in Japan, to develop AI solutions for Japan's needs, and democratize AI in Japan.

Leadership

David Ha

Co-Founder & CEO

Former Research Scientist at Google, where he led the Japan Research Team at Google Brain. His primary research areas include complex self-organizing systems and the self-evolution of artificial intelligence.

Team

Research

We share unique, cutting-edge research inspired by the collective intelligence of nature — including The AI Scientist, Darwin Gödel Machine, ShinkaEvolve, and AB-MCTS.

Applied

At the frontier of finance, defense, and intelligence, we draw out tacit knowledge into AI, combine multiple models, and design and implement solutions that reach directly into Japan's social infrastructure.

Product

We develop and deliver user-facing AI products — including Sakana Chat, Sakana Marlin, and Sakana Fugu — translating cutting-edge research into accessible tools.

Corporate

We support Sakana AI's mission through finance, legal, HR, and operations, building the foundation to keep running at the frontier of AI research.

Logo

Sakana AI logo is inspired by creatures in nature, such as schools of fish. Just as fish move together and adapt fluidly to their environment, Sakana AI incorporates this concept of "collective intelligence" into its AI development, pursuing a new type of generative AI model that can adapt and respond to its surroundings.
