FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a guarantee on the number of domains or backlinks acquired through Link Building（Link Earning）?

Our Backlink Building (Backlink Earning) service does not offer guarantees on the number of backlinks acquired. Currently, the number of backlinks is not the only important factor in referring domains and backlinks. Blindly pursuing a high number of backlinks can result in links from low-quality sites and, in some cases, may even lead to penalties.

How long does it take to see results from Link Building（Link Earning）?

The time it takes to see results from Backlink Building (Backlink Earning) strategies varies depending on the project scale, industry, and competitive landscape. Generally, improvements in search rankings and increases in organic search traffic can be observed within 3-6 months after starting the initiatives. However, continuous implementation of strategies can lead to greater long-term effects.

What is the contract period for your Link Building（Link Earning） service?

Our company offers contracts starting from a 6-month period.

How do you measure domain strength?

We analyze domain authority (domain power or domain rating) based on metrics from tools like Ahrefs and Semrush. However, as these tools' metrics may not always be accurate, we primarily commit to acquiring backlinks from high-quality and relevant domains through our Backlink Building (Backlink Earning) strategies. As a result, domain authority may improve over time.
